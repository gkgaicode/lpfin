## Core Tech Stack & Focus Areas
The company manages over $2.4 Trillion in Assets under Management (AUM) and relies heavily on complex fintech software. In their tech hub, your work will primarily center around: [4, 5] 

* 
* Next-Gen FinTech: Wealth management platforms, client portals, and algorithmic compliance engines.
* Core Modern Tech: Artificial intelligence engineering, advanced data analytics platforms, cybersecurity, and cloud-native middleware.
* AI-Driven QA: Integration of AI (like GitHub Copilot) for code generation, test automation, and early-stage bug prediction. [4, 5, 6, 7]

## The Pros for Software Engineers

* 
* Good Work-Life Balance: Most developers report a flexible culture with high marks (around 4.6/5) for maintaining proper working boundaries. [2, 8] 
* Strong Engineering Talent: You will work alongside experienced fintech architects with a clear product roadmap. [8, 9] 
* Greenfield Growth: Since the India GCC is scaling up rapidly to hit a target of 2,500 techies, early joiners have a distinct advantage in building out new teams. [4, 5]

## The Cons to Keep in Mind

*
